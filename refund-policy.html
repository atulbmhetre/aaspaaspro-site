Refund & Cancellation Policy

App: Aaspaas Pro  ·  Last updated: 17 August 2026
Because payments for orders happen directly between customers and vendors, most refunds and cancellations are handled within the app, according to the rules below.

1. Customer-to-Vendor Orders

Aaspaas Pro does not hold or process customer-to-vendor payments, so we cannot directly issue refunds for orders. Cancellation rules and any resulting bill adjustment are handled automatically in the app, as described below.

2. When You Can Cancel an Order

Service typeCancellation allowed
HelpAny time until the vendor has started the service (e.g. marked "I've Started"). Not available after that point.
Delivery (scheduled slot)Any time before the delivery window begins.
Delivery (ASAP / immediate)Only before the vendor accepts. Not available once accepted, since dispatch may begin immediately.
AppointmentGoverned by the booking's own cancellation rules shown at the time of booking.

Once a valid cancellation is made, any unpaid bill tied to that order is automatically voided.

3. If You Can No Longer Cancel

Once a service or delivery is underway, cancellation is no longer available in-app — please contact the vendor directly using the in-app call option to resolve the situation.

4. Payment Disputes

If a customer marks a payment as made and the vendor does not agree, either party can raise it within the app's order flow for resolution. Aaspaas Pro does not reverse or process payments directly, as it does not hold customer funds. A pattern of disputed payments on an account may result in that account being limited to cash-only payment, to protect vendors.

5. Vendor Subscription Payments

Where SAMTech charges vendors a subscription or platform fee (processed via Razorpay), such payments are non-refundable except where required by applicable law, or as explicitly stated at the time of purchase.
