What Aaspaas Pro does

Aaspaas Pro connects customers with vendors already inside their own neighborhood, across three service modes:

HelpOn-the-spot local service, at your place or theirs.
DeliveryOrder from local shops, delivered nearby.
AppointmentBook a slot with a local business.

Every vendor is discoverable only within a genuine local radius — the person accepting your order is realistically close enough to deliver, visit, or host you.
